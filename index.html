Establishing Agency & Authorization

We establish legal agency to initiate the audit. This aligns carrier compliance protocols and clears security checks for seamless data access.

  • >
    Letter of Authorization (LOA): You provide a standard, single-page document on corporate letterhead. This designates our firm as your authorized agent to audit invoices, request documentation, and file billing disputes.
  • >
    Deployment: We bind this credential to all initial carrier communications. This secures immediate, validated access to necessary billing records.

Operational & Commercial Parameters

The Master Service Agreement (MSA) defines the operational scope, locks performance metrics, and protects your internal corporate data.

  • >
    Contingency Model: We operate on a pure performance metric, retaining 30% of successfully realized recoveries. Zero upfront costs. Zero ongoing software fees. If we locate no variance, you incur no charges.
  • >
    Data Security (NDA): A strict non-disclosure agreement locks the absolute confidentiality of your supply chain routing, rate matrices, and financial architecture.
  • >
    Term Structure: Standardized terms establish the lifecycle of the partnership. Clear definitions dictate termination protocols for either entity at any time.

Capital Routing & Resolution

Our recovery model is decentralized. We do not hold client funds in escrow. This eliminates financial liability, simplifies internal accounting, and accelerates access to recovered capital.

  • >
    Detection & Execution: The system identifies billing anomalies and compiles supporting evidence. We transmit a fully substantiated dispute to the carrier alongside your LOA.
  • >
    Direct Account Crediting: We instruct the carrier to apply credit memos directly to your active carrier accounts. For historical audits, physical refund checks are routed directly to your corporate address.
  • >
    Reconciliation & Invoicing: At the conclusion of the monthly cycle, we generate a precise variance report documenting all finalized credits. We transmit an invoice exclusively for our 30% allocation of confirmed recovered value.
