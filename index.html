Structural Inefficiencies

Carrier contracts are inherently complex,
resulting in systemic capital leakage.

01.

Contractual Drift

Negotiated base rates serve as a theoretical baseline. Complex carrier matrices frequently diverge from executed contracts, applying uncontracted lanes, expired tariffs, or failing to honor established volume tiers.

02.

Accessorial Leakage

The accessorial schedule is the leading source of billing variance. Fuel schedule mismatches and duplicate terminal handling charges routinely fall outside standard AP review thresholds.

03.

The AP Dilemma

Standard internal workflows prioritize operational speed over forensic accuracy. Without bandwidth for line-item historical reconciliation against multi-tiered tariffs, finance teams automate capital leakage.
