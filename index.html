Structural Inefficiencies

Carrier contracts are complex by design, leading to systemic capital leakage.

01.

Contractual Drift

Negotiated base rates act merely as a theoretical baseline. Complex carrier matrices frequently diverge from executed contracts, applying uncontracted lanes, expired tariffs, or failing to honor established volume tiers.

02.

Accessorial Hemorrhage

The accessorial schedule represents the primary extraction vector. Carriers deploy obfuscated fuel schedule mismatches and duplicate terminal handling charges that routinely bypass standard AP approval tolerances.

03.

The AP Dilemma

Standard internal workflows prioritize operational speed over forensic accuracy. Without bandwidth for line-item historical reconciliation against multi-tiered tariffs, finance effectively automates capital hemorrhage.
